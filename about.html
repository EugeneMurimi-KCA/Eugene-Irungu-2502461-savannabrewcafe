Our story

One small machine. A very Kenyan dream.

Savanna Brew Café opened in 2019 with one small espresso machine and a big dream: to celebrate Kenyan coffee. Founder Amina Otieno grew up on her grandparents' coffee farm in Nyeri and wanted to bring that flavour to the city.

Today the café supports local farmers, uses eco-friendly packaging and hosts community events every month. We believe a great café should do more than serve a good cup — it should bring people together.

  • Beans sourced from trusted growers in Nyeri and Kiambu
  • Small-batch roasting to protect each origin’s character
  • Every drink prepared fresh, never rushed
“I wanted to create a place where people could experience the richness of Kenyan coffee and feel at home, one cup at a time.”— Amina Otieno, Founder
